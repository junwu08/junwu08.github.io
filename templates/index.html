About Me

I received my Ph.D. degree from Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech, 南方科技大学) in June 2026, under the supervision of Dr. Weijie Yuan. My research is focused on wireless communications and signal processing, particularly the following topics:

  • Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) ☘️
  • OTFS modulation and delay-Doppler signal processing ☘️
  • Low-altitude wireless networks (LAWNs) ☘️
  • UAV cooperative sensing and trajectory design ☘️
  • Control-aware beamforming and wireless resource allocation ☘️

For academic collaboration 🤝, please feel free to contact me at wuj2021@mail.sustech.edu.cn 📩.

Personal Experience

I received my B.E. degree from Southwest Jiaotong University (西南交通大学), Chengdu, China, in 2021, and my Ph.D. degree from Southern University of Science and Technology, Shenzhen, China, in 2026, supervised by Dr. Weijie Yuan.

From July to September 2023, I was a Visiting Student at Xi'an Jiaotong University (西安交通大学), supervised by Prof. Zhiqiang Wei. From June to September 2025, I was a Visiting Student at Sungkyunkwan University (成均馆大学), Suwon, South Korea, supervised by Prof. Dong In Kim, IEEE Life Fellow.

My work spans ISAC signal processing, OTFS precoding, cooperative sensing, and the joint design of communication and control for UAV networks. My first-author publications appear in IEEE JSAC, IEEE TWC, IEEE TMC, IEEE TVT, IEEE IoT Journal, IEEE Wireless Communications, China Communications, and Chinese Journal of Aeronautics.

I received the IEEE WCNC Best Paper Award in 2026, the Radar Journal Annual Excellent Paper Award in 2025, the Chinese National Scholarship and BYD Scholarship in 2025, and the IEEE TGCN Exemplary Reviewer recognition in 2022.
